# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics — Embodied Intelligence in the Physical World

## Overview

Welcome to the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics course. This educational resource bridges the gap between digital intelligence and physical embodiment, focusing on how humanoid robotics serves as a platform for understanding embodied intelligence.

## Goals

This course aims to:
- Introduce the fundamental concepts of Physical AI and embodied intelligence
- Explain the architecture and components of robotic systems
- Demonstrate how robots perceive and interact with the physical world
- Showcase practical applications of Variable Level of Autonomy (VLA) systems

## Prerequisites

To get the most out of this course, you should have:
- A background in Computer Science, AI, or Software Engineering
- Basic understanding of machine learning and artificial intelligence concepts
- Familiarity with programming concepts (no specific language required)

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will:
- Understand the distinction between digital AI and Physical AI
- Grasp the importance of humanoid form factors in robotics research
- Know how robotic systems are architected and coordinated
- Comprehend how robots perceive their environment and make decisions
- Recognize practical applications of Physical AI in autonomous systems
